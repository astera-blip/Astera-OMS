# 退款 SOP

## Purpose

Handle refunds without overwriting original payment or finance records.

## Draft Steps

1. Identify refund source: cancellation, official refund, shortage, damaged item, or manual adjustment.
2. Confirm refund amount.
3. Decide refund destination according to final policy.
4. Create Refund record.
5. Create negative or corrective ledger entries where needed.
6. If moved to wallet, create Wallet Transaction.
7. Record admin actor, reason, and related order items.

## Checks

- Do not delete original payment.
- Do not overwrite historical profit sharing.
- Formal refund policy requires owner and legal review.
