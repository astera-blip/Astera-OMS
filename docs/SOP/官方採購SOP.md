# 官方採購 SOP

## Purpose

Record official purchase outcomes without losing partial success, shortage, or waitlist information.

## Draft Steps

1. Open orders or order items pending official purchase.
2. Purchase from official source.
3. Record successful purchased items.
4. Record failed or unavailable items.
5. For partial success, keep successful order items and update failed items according to campaign rules.
6. Notify customer of actual purchased items and amount when notification feature exists.
7. Keep purchase evidence private.

## Checks

- Do not replace one order item with another product.
- Preserve original customer request time for waitlist order.
- Create case if official error or exception occurs.
