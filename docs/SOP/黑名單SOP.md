# 黑名單 SOP

## Purpose

Manage visible and hidden risk information without exposing internal CRM data to customers.

## Draft Steps

1. Identify reason for risk note or blacklist.
2. Decide whether this is internal note, hidden risk flag, or formal blacklist.
3. Record reason and evidence.
4. Apply restrictions only for formal blacklist status.
5. Keep history when blacklist is removed.
6. Ensure customer-facing pages do not expose internal status.

## Checks

- Hidden risk notes are admin-only.
- Formal blacklist history should remain auditable.
- The exact policy requires owner review.
