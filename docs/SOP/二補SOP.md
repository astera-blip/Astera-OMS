# 二補 SOP

## Purpose

Create and manage supplement payment requests after arrival or shipping-cost calculation.

## Draft Steps

1. Select arrived order items requiring supplement payment.
2. Calculate international shipping, domestic shipping, platform fees, and extra costs.
3. Create Supplement and Supplement Items.
4. Generate Payment Request.
5. Notify customer when notification feature exists.
6. Track due date and reminders.
7. Confirm payment through payment SOP.

## Checks

- Large abnormal amounts should trigger warning.
- Thresholds and reminder timing require owner decision.
- Wallet usage must preserve any minimum payment amount required by third-party platform rules.
