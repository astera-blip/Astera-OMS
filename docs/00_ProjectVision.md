# Astera OMS Project Vision

Astera OMS is planned as a Thai GL and artist merchandise preorder commerce system plus internal operations workspace. It is not only a storefront. The long-term product includes OMS, PIM, CRM, finance, reporting, and daily work management.

## Confirmed Direction

- Build a safe public-test MVP first.
- Use the MVP as the first layer of the real architecture, not a throwaway prototype.
- Keep backend-managed content out of hardcoded frontend code.
- Preserve historical order, payment, cost, and consent records.
- Add modules gradually instead of repeatedly rewriting the system.
- Do not add third-party services unless the purpose, cost, alternatives, and maintenance impact are clear.

## MVP Goal

The first milestone is:

> Customers can sign in, browse products, place orders, and the owner can safely manage products, members, orders, and manual payment confirmation.

## Long-Term Modules

- Storefront
- Product Information Management
- Order Management
- Member and CRM
- Payment and receivables
- Arrival, supplement payment, and shipping
- Finance, ledger, and profit sharing
- Cases and exception handling
- Workspace task center
- Notifications and reports
