# Legal and Privacy Draft

This file is an operational draft, not legal advice. Formal terms and privacy policy must be reviewed before public launch.

## Required Legal Documents

- Member service terms.
- Purchasing service terms.
- Payment and supplement payment rules.
- Refund and cancellation rules.
- Waitlist rules.
- After-sales rules.
- Privacy policy.

## Consent Timing

### Member Registration

Customer should agree to:

- Member service terms.
- Privacy policy.

### Checkout

Customer should agree to:

- The specific purchasing service terms for the sale campaign.
- Payment deadline.
- Possible official delays.
- Supplement payment possibility.
- Cancellation and after-sales rules.
- Unboxing video requirement if adopted.

## Consent Records

The system should store:

- Member UID.
- Order ID when consent is order-related.
- Legal document version.
- Consent time.
- IP or request metadata if legally and technically appropriate.
- Immutable version reference or content snapshot.

## Privacy Data Classes

Personal and sensitive operational data includes:

- Name
- Phone
- Email
- Address
- Community ID
- Order records
- Payment records
- Bank reconciliation files
- Blacklist and internal notes
- Customer service cases
- Private attachments

## Legal Review Required

Do not assume these clauses are automatically enforceable:

- All purchasing items excluded from seven-day cooling-off period.
- Official failure always means no refund.
- All risk fully transferred to the customer.
- Store keeps final interpretation rights.

Terms should be fair, transparent, and readable.
